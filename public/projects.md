## Featured

### KERB - Preserve the Curve (2026)
AI-powered motorsport intelligence system that identifies potential track-limit violations in race footage and generates evidence packages for human stewards. Combines YOLOv12, ByteTrack, vehicle geometry, temporal filtering, and trust scoring. A steward-assist system, never an automatic referee.
Tech: Next.js, Python, YOLOv12, ByteTrack, OpenCV, FFmpeg, Google Colab
Links: https://github.com/PrithviKaushik/kerb · Demo: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nYmELOBlbnM

### SIF-Trace (2026)
An explainable AI/NLP system that identifies Serious Injury and Fatality (SIF) precursors hidden in industrial safety reports. Uses an LLM to extract semantic facts and a deterministic safety engine to evaluate critical barriers, reason about their states, and generate evidence-backed safety intelligence. Selected for the Smart India Hackathon 2026 National Final Round.
Tech: React, TypeScript, Vite, Python, REST API, Gemini LLM, Custom SIF Ontology, Deterministic Safety Engine

### Threadly (2026)
An AI-powered relationship intelligence platform that helps individuals and teams build, manage, and maintain meaningful connections. Features a coordinated crew of AI agents for contact enrichment, personalized follow-ups, email qualification, social conversation monitoring, and meeting intelligence.
Tech: AI Agents, LLMs, Relationship Intelligence, Workflow Automation, NLP, Multi-Agent Systems
Links: https://threadly1-gules.vercel.app · https://github.com/Waqar080206/threadly1

### LapLogic (2026)
A Web3-powered Formula 1 fantasy strategy game built around the 2026 season. 3 drivers and 1 constructor under a 60M GameCoin budget, AI-generated race prediction challenges, Polygon smart-contract payouts, wallet integration, and no-upfront-crypto onboarding.
Tech: Next.js, React, TypeScript, Node.js, Solidity, ethers.js, MongoDB, MetaMask, Web3
Links: https://fullofwater.vercel.app · https://github.com/Waqar080206/fullofwater
